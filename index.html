How tokens work
  • 📅 Log in every day for 1 🪙. Every day Monday–Saturday unlocks Sunday's chest: 3–5 🪙 (1 if you missed a day).
  • ⚔️ Daily quests are worth 4 🪙: "check out" quests pay 1, "do something" quests pay 2. Finish one, then tap Claim — unclaimed rewards don't expire.
  • 🎲 Finishing today's puzzle in Games pays 1 🪙 straight away.
  • 🛒 The Store has token packs, and Supporter plans that add 80 or 180 🪙 a month.
  • 🎁 Spend them in a Secret Giveaway: every token you drop in is one more chance on top of your free one. They're used up when the winners are drawn, win or lose, and come back if you take them out before.

Isabella the Warrior

EpicHumanHeroMelee

Isabella the Warrior is an Epic Human hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. At level 1, it has 24,000 HP and 925 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 42,500 HP and 1,375 attack — 77% more HP and 49% more attack. Its ability is Vengeance.

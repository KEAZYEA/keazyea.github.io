Storm Mistresses

EpicMageTroopMelee

Storm Mistresses are an Epic Mage troop in Kingdom Cash that fight in melee. Experienced warriors who know how to tame the full power of storms. Fully elusive on the battlefield. At level 1, they have 1,100 HP and 120 attack. By level 10 (their maximum), that grows to 21,550 HP and 1,090 attack — 19.6× the HP and 9.1× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up.

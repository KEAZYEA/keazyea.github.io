Bumi the Dreamwalker

LegendaryMageHeroRangedArea damage

Bumi the Dreamwalker is a Legendary Mage hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 24,750 HP and 540 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 38,250 HP and 675 attack — 55% more HP and 25% more attack. Its ability is Dream, and its talent is Sand Storm.

Dream

Bumi creates sandy areas that cast enemies into sleep. Enemies can't attack and use their abilities during the dream state. Dream ends when enemies receive the first damage.

Cooldown 16 s at level 1 to 14 s at level 10; area radius 4 at level 1 to 8 at level 10.

Imp

RareUndeadTroopRanged

Imp is a Rare Undead troop in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. Vile creatures resurrected by some crazy magician. They're ideal for bombarding the enemy from the air. At level 1, it has 1,800 HP and 615 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 42,840 HP and 5,250 attack — 23.8× the HP and 8.5× the attack. The squad grows from 1 to 3 units as it levels up.

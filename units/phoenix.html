Phoenix

LegendaryMageTroopRangedArea damage

Phoenix is a Legendary Mage troop in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range, dealing area damage. A legendary fire bird that can be reborn from the ashes. At level 1, it has 1,500 HP and 150 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 45,000 HP and 465 attack — 30× the HP and 3.1× the attack. A squad has 1 unit. Its ability is Rebirth.

Ability

Rebirth

Upon taking fatal damage, the Phoenix turns into a burning egg that explodes and deals damage to enemies. After the explosion, the Phoenix is reborn.

Damage 300 at level 1 to 600 at level 10; area radius 3 at level 1 to 5 at level 10.

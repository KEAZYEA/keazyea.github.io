Atreya, Hand of Vengeance

MythicElevateHeroRanged

Atreya, Hand of Vengeance is a Mythic Elevate hero in Kingdom Clash that attacks from range. At level 1, it has 28,500 HP and 700 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 57,750 HP and 1,060 attack — 103% more HP and 51% more attack. Its ability is Wrath of Heaven, and its talent is Smashing Light.

Bone Dragon

LegendaryUndeadHeroRangedArea damage

Bone Dragon is a Legendary Undead hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 28,000 HP and 530 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 64,500 HP and 660 attack — 130% more HP and 25% more attack. Its ability is Death Flight, and its talent is Death Aura.

Huntress

LegendaryHumanTroopRanged

Huntress is a Legendary Human troop in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. Carries two weapons: one for monsters and one for men. At level 1, it has 2,000 HP and 485 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 65,000 HP and 2,370 attack — 32.5× the HP and 4.9× the attack. A squad has 1 unit. Its ability is Powerful Salvo.

Air Elemental

EpicMageTroopMelee

Air Elemental is an Epic Mage troop in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. The very embodiment of air, capable of making any head spin. At level 1, it has 1,000 HP and 110 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 21,100 HP and 990 attack — 21.1× the HP and 9× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up. Its ability is Vortex.

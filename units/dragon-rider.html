Dragon Rider

LegendaryHumanHeroRangedArea damage

Dragon Rider is a Legendary Human hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 27,000 HP and 375 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 61,000 HP and 560 attack — 126% more HP and 49% more attack. Its ability is Meteor Strike, and its talent is Aerial Support.

Edelina, Queen of the Forest

LegendaryMageHeroRanged

Edelina, Queen of the Forest is a Legendary Mage hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. At level 1, it has 23,500 HP and 650 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 34,750 HP and 870 attack — 48% more HP and 34% more attack. Its ability is Spiked Roots, and its talent is Thorns.

Gravedigger

RareUndeadTroopMelee

Gravedigger is a Rare Undead troop in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. They like to dig into the ground near less popular roads and attack lone travelers. At level 1, it has 850 HP and 100 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 18,000 HP and 975 attack — 21.2× the HP and 9.8× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up. Its ability is Underground Passage.

Remus the Indestructible

MythicElevateHeroMeleeArea damage

Remus the Indestructible is a Mythic Elevate hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 50,250 HP and 700 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 95,250 HP and 1,375 attack — 90% more HP and 96% more attack. Its ability is Light Shield, and its talent is Threat of Giants.

Talent: Threat of Giants

Remus powers up the regular attacks of allied troops to deal more damage to enemy tanks and bosses. The effect continues after the hero's death.

Increased damage dealt: +30% at talent level 1, up to +85% at level 10.

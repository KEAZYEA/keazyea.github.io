Remus the Indestructible

MythicElevateHeroMeleeArea damage

Remus the Indestructible is a Mythic Elevate hero in Kingdom Clash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 50,250 HP and 700 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 95,250 HP and 1,375 attack — 90% more HP and 96% more attack. Its ability is Light Shield, and its talent is Threat of Giants.

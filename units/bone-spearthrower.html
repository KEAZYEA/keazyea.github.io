Bone Spearthrower

CommonUndeadTroopRanged

Bone Spearthrower is a Common Undead troop in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. He throws spears no worse than the living. Each new throw increases the flow of recruits to the undead army. At level 1, it has 700 HP and 115 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 15,280 HP and 1,350 attack — 21.8× the HP and 11.7× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up.

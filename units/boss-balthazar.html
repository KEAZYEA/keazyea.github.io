Balthazar

BossFire DragonLevels 1–300

Balthazar, the Fire Dragon, is a Glorious Hunt boss in Kingdom Clash, from level 1 to 300. It attacks with Basic attack, Fire Breath, Fury from the Deep, Shattering Strikes. Its passive abilities are Sharp Growths and Smooth Scales. Pushing it all the way to level 300 takes 37,556,733 damage in total.

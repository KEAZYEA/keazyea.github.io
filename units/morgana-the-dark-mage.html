Morgana the Dark Mage

LegendaryUndeadHeroRanged

Morgana the Dark Mage is a Legendary Undead hero in Kingdom Clash that attacks from range. At level 1, it has 22,500 HP and 760 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 33,750 HP and 1,030 attack — 50% more HP and 36% more attack. Its ability is Mysterious Tombstone, and its talent is Unholy Alliance.

Morgana the Dark Mage

LegendaryUndeadHeroRanged

Morgana the Dark Mage is a Legendary Undead hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. At level 1, it has 22,500 HP and 760 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 33,750 HP and 1,030 attack — 50% more HP and 36% more attack. Its ability is Mysterious Tombstone, and its talent is Unholy Alliance.

Ability

Mysterious Tombstone

Morgana summons a tombstone that taunts enemy units to attack it. After it's destroyed, skeletons burst from it. While the taunt is active, enemy units can't use their abilities.

Cooldown 16 s at level 1 to 14 s at level 10.

Talent: Unholy Alliance

Morgana buffs all summoned allied skeletons, increasing their attack and HP. With each of her attacks, Morgana curses her enemies to rise in the form of allied skeletons after they die.

Increased damage dealt: +4% at talent level 1, up to +40% at level 10.

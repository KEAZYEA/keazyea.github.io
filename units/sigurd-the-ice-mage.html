Sigurd the Ice Mage

LegendaryMageHeroRanged

Sigurd the Ice Mage is a Legendary Mage hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. At level 1, it has 25,500 HP and 800 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 36,750 HP and 1,160 attack — 44% more HP and 45% more attack. Its ability is Snowball, and its talent is Frost Armor.

Ability

Snowball

Sigurd creates a huge snowball that speeds forward, increasing in size. The snowball sweeps up any enemy units in its path. At the end of its path, the snowball smashes open, and the units in it are damaged and stunned.

Damage 750 at level 1 to 1,425 at level 10; stun 1 s at level 1 to 3 s at level 10; cooldown 16 s at level 1 to 14 s at level 10; max targets 10 at level 1 to 55 at level 10.

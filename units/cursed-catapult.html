Cursed Catapult

RareUndeadTroopRangedArea damage

Cursed Catapult is a Rare Undead troop in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range, dealing area damage. An undead army trophy catapult strengthened by dark magic. Sometimes uses skeletons as projectiles for sudden attacks from behind enemy lines. At level 1, it has 1,000 HP and 210 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 9,000 HP and 1,440 attack — 9× the HP and 6.9× the attack. A squad has 1 unit. Its ability is Skeleton Shot.

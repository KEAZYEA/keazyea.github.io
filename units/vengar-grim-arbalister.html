Vengar, Grim Arbalister

RareUndeadHeroRanged

Vengar, Grim Arbalister is a Rare Undead hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. At level 1, it has 18,000 HP and 550 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 29,250 HP and 770 attack — 63% more HP and 40% more attack. Its ability is Shadow Attack.

Battle Golem

CommonMageTroopMelee

Battle Golem is a Common Mage troop in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. Artificial creatures brought to life by magic. Wizards use these soulless golems as primary melee troops. At level 1, it has 950 HP and 105 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 17,540 HP and 800 attack — 18.5× the HP and 7.6× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up.

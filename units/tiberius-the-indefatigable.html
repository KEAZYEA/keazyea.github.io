Tiberius the Indefatigable

EpicHumanHeroMeleeArea damage

Tiberius the Indefatigable is an Epic Human hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 30,000 HP and 650 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 64,000 HP and 875 attack — 113% more HP and 35% more attack. Its ability is Battering Ram.

Ability

Battering Ram

Combines two shields and makes a powerful dash forward, dealing damage to all enemies on its path. At the end of the dash, all enemies in a large radius are pushed back, damaged and stunned.

Damage 250 at level 1 to 500 at level 10; stun 1 s at level 1 to 4 s at level 10; cooldown 16 s at level 1 to 14 s at level 10; area radius 5 at level 1 to 9 at level 10.

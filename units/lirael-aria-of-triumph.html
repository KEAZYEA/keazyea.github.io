Lirael, Aria of Triumph

LegendaryMageHeroMeleeArea damage

Lirael, Aria of Triumph is a Legendary Mage hero in Kingdom Clash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 24,750 HP and 540 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 38,250 HP and 675 attack — 55% more HP and 25% more attack. Its ability is Song of Courage, and its talent is Power of Unity.

General

RareHumanHeroMelee

General is a Rare Human hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. At level 1, it has 20,000 HP and 330 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 31,250 HP and 530 attack — 56% more HP and 61% more attack. Its ability is Area Strike.

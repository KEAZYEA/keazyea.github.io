Iron Guards

RareHumanTroopMelee

Iron Guards are a Rare Human troop in Kingdom Cash that fight in melee. Protect iron from corrosion. At level 1, they have 1,000 HP and 90 attack. By level 10 (their maximum), that grows to 16,800 HP and 835 attack — 16.8× the HP and 9.3× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up.

Monk

EpicHumanTroopMelee

Monk is an Epic Human troop in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. Monks' deceptive appearance hides dangerous fighters. Quick strikes to weak points help monks to defeat any opponent. At level 1, it has 1,200 HP and 100 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 23,310 HP and 840 attack — 19.4× the HP and 8.4× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up.

A.D.E.P.T.

LegendaryHumanTroopMelee

A.D.E.P.T. is a Legendary Human troop in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. Battle machine created by human engineers to combat dangerous monsters. At level 1, it has 2,500 HP and 200 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 100,000 HP and 2,500 attack — 40× the HP and 12.5× the attack. A squad has 1 unit. Its ability is Impenetrable shield.

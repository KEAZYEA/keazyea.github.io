Charon the Blazing

RareUndeadHeroMelee

Charon the Blazing is a Rare Undead hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. At level 1, it has 23,500 HP and 675 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 34,750 HP and 900 attack — 48% more HP and 33% more attack. Its ability is Cursed Army.

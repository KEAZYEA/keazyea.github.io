Mortimer the Damned

EpicUndeadHeroMeleeArea damage

Mortimer the Damned is an Epic Undead hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 27,000 HP and 500 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 61,000 HP and 725 attack — 126% more HP and 45% more attack. Its ability is Death Grip.

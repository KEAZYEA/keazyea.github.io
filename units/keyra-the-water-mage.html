Keyra the Water Mage

LegendaryMageHeroRangedArea damage

Keyra the Water Mage is a Legendary Mage hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 24,750 HP and 560 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 36,900 HP and 690 attack — 49% more HP and 23% more attack. Its ability is Howl of the Deep, and its talent is Power of Water.

Ability

Howl of the Deep

Keyra creates powerful geysers that toss enemy units into the air. Units tossed up by geysers take damage and are unable to attack or use abilities until they land.

Damage 200 at level 1 to 650 at level 10; cooldown 16 s at level 1 to 14 s at level 10; area radius 3.

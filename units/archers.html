Archers

CommonHumanTroopRanged

Archers are a Common Human troop in Kingdom Clash that attack from range. Good helpers for infantry. At level 1, they have 600 HP and 195 attack. By level 10 (their maximum), that grows to 14,280 HP and 1,550 attack — 23.8× the HP and 7.9× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up.

Steel Revenant

LegendaryUndeadTroopMelee

Steel Revenant is a Legendary Undead troop in Kingdom Clash that fights in melee. A centuries-old curse has granted this mighty warrior sharp spikes and condemned him to eternal solitude. At level 1, it has 4,400 HP and 325 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 100,650 HP and 1,955 attack — 22.9× the HP and 6× the attack. A squad has 1 unit.

Giant Toad

LegendaryMageTroopMelee

Giant Toad is a Legendary Mage troop in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. This little cutie may mistake you for a fly and eat you. At level 1, it has 3,600 HP and 340 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 69,930 HP and 2,620 attack — 19.4× the HP and 7.7× the attack. A squad has 1 unit. Its ability is Concussive Hop.

Shaman

LegendaryMageTroopRanged

Shaman is a Legendary Mage troop in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. Two options: he'll either save the village from drought or not. Shaman attacks six enemies at once with chain lightning. At level 1, it has 2,000 HP and 120 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 65,000 HP and 470 attack — 32.5× the HP and 3.9× the attack. A squad has 1 unit.

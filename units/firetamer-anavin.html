Firetamer Anavin

LegendaryMageHeroRangedArea damage

Firetamer Anavin is a Legendary Mage hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 24,750 HP and 390 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 35,100 HP and 570 attack — 42% more HP and 46% more attack. Its abilities are Rebirth and Rise of the Phoenix, and its talent is Flaming Heart.

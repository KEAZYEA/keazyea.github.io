Brutallus the Terrorbringer

MythicDemonHeroMeleeArea damage

Brutallus the Terrorbringer is a Mythic Demon hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 50,250 HP and 775 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 105,500 HP and 1,500 attack — 110% more HP and 94% more attack. Its ability is Primal Fear, and its talent is Easy Prey.

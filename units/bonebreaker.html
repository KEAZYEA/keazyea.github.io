Bonebreaker

LegendaryHumanTroopMelee

Bonebreaker is a Legendary Human troop in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. Bonebreakers always hold back their rage. Only battles help them realize their full potential! At level 1, it has 1,800 HP and 150 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 42,840 HP and 1,420 attack — 23.8× the HP and 9.5× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up.

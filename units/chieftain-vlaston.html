Chieftain Vlaston

EpicHumanHeroMeleeArea damage

Chieftain Vlaston is an Epic Human hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 27,000 HP and 700 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 61,000 HP and 925 attack — 126% more HP and 32% more attack. Its ability is Furious Attack.

Furious Attack

Vlaston puts all his power into a devastating hammer blow that starts a small earthquake. This ability deals damage and stuns enemies.

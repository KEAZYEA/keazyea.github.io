Bombers

RareHumanTroopRangedArea damage

Bombers are a Rare Human troop in Kingdom Cash that attack from range, dealing area damage. Blowing things up is our job! At level 1, they have 500 HP and 140 attack. By level 10 (their maximum), that grows to 13,020 HP and 1,425 attack — 26× the HP and 10.2× the attack. A squad has 1 unit.

Swordmaster

RareHumanHeroMelee

Swordmaster is a Rare Human hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. At level 1, it has 22,500 HP and 700 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 33,750 HP and 925 attack — 50% more HP and 32% more attack. Its ability is Earth's Breakup.

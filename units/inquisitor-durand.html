Inquisitor Durand

LegendaryHumanHeroMeleeArea damage

Inquisitor Durand is a Legendary Human hero in Kingdom Clash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 27,000 HP and 650 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 80,000 HP and 1,000 attack — 196% more HP and 54% more attack. Its ability is Righteous Flame, and its talent is Immutability.

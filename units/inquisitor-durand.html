Inquisitor Durand

LegendaryHumanHeroMeleeArea damage

Inquisitor Durand is a Legendary Human hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 27,000 HP and 650 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 80,000 HP and 1,000 attack — 196% more HP and 54% more attack. Its ability is Righteous Flame, and its talent is Immutability.

Righteous Flame

Durand throws a burning thurible that releases a wave of fire when it hits the ground. Enemy units hit by the wave take periodic damage and run away in fear.

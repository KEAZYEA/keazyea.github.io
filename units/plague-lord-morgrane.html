Plague Lord Morgrane

LegendaryUndeadHeroMeleeArea damage

Plague Lord Morgrane is a Legendary Undead hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 27,000 HP and 700 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 80,000 HP and 1,060 attack — 196% more HP and 51% more attack. Its ability is Plague Rat Summon, and its talent is Black Plague.

Ability

Plague Rat Summon

Morgrane summons a plague rat that marks the enemy with each attack. When a marked unit dies, the hero's ability cooldown is reduced. When the rat is killed, it releases a plague cloud that also marks all enemies.

Cooldown 16 s at level 1 to 12 s at level 10.

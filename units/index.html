Kingdom Cash troops & heroes

A guide to every unit in Kingdom Cash: 36 heroes and 39 troops across the 5 factions (Human, Undead, Mage, Elevate, Demon). Each page lists the unit's HP, attack, defence and power at every level, 53 units' abilities with their damage, cooldown and area numbers level by level, and each hero's talent and what it adds as it levels up.

Heroes are single, powerful units with abilities and talents; troops fight as squads that grow as they level up. Rarity runs from Common through Rare, Epic and Legendary up to Mythic. To compare two units side by side, build a formation or check the latest promo codes, open the app.

Zeirath the Lord of Lightning

EpicMageHeroRanged

Zeirath the Lord of Lightning is an Epic Mage hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. At level 1, it has 19,250 HP and 690 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 30,500 HP and 1,050 attack — 58% more HP and 52% more attack. Its ability is Lightning Storm.

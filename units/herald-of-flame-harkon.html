Herald of Flame Harkon

MythicDemonHeroRangedArea damage

Herald of Flame Harkon is a Mythic Demon hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 28,500 HP and 700 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 57,750 HP and 1,060 attack — 103% more HP and 51% more attack. Its ability is Insatiable Flame, and its talent is Echo of Pain.

Ability

Insatiable Flame

Harkon shoots a fireball that deals damage and ignites the area where it lands. Each enemy troop that dies in the burning area increases the strength of the fireballs.

Damage 1,400 at level 1 to 3,800 at level 10; cooldown 8 s at level 1 to 4 s at level 10; area radius 4 at level 1 to 6 at level 10.

Talent: Echo of Pain

Harkon enchants allied units so they only take a portion of incoming damage. The remaining damage is taken gradually once per second for 5 seconds. The effect continues after the hero's death. The talent does not work against boss attacks and abilities.

Delayed Damage: +20% at talent level 1, up to +56% at level 10.

Ophelia the Spellcaster

LegendaryMageHeroRanged

Ophelia the Spellcaster is a Legendary Mage hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. At level 1, it has 22,500 HP and 350 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 33,750 HP and 570 attack — 50% more HP and 63% more attack. Its ability is Summon Spirit, and its talent is Revenge of the Fallen.

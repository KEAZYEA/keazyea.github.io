Shai-Khold

BossIce SnakeLevels 1–300

Shai-Khold, the Ice Snake, is a Glorious Hunt boss in Kingdom Clash, from level 1 to 300. It attacks with Frost Needles and Summon Ice Guardians. Pushing it all the way to level 300 takes 37,556,733 damage in total.

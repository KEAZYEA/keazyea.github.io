Bjorn the Druid

EpicMageHeroMeleeArea damage

Bjorn the Druid is an Epic Mage hero in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 24,000 HP and 650 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 42,500 HP and 875 attack — 77% more HP and 35% more attack. Its ability is Spirit Form.

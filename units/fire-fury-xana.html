Fire Fury Xana

MythicDemonHeroRangedArea damage

Fire Fury Xana is a Mythic Demon hero in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range, dealing area damage. At level 1, it has 23,000 HP and 700 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 55,250 HP and 1,100 attack — 140% more HP and 57% more attack. Its ability is Inferno, and its talent is Meteor Requiem.

Ability

Inferno

Xana creates infernal zones on the battlefield that burn enemies and rain down meteorites on their heads. Each large meteorite deals damage and turns into a lava golem when it falls.

Damage 200 at level 1 to 500 at level 10; cooldown 19 s at level 1 to 16 s at level 10; area radius 4 at level 1 to 6 at level 10.

Talent: Meteor Requiem

The last allied unit in a troop to die summons a meteorite that crashes down at their location and deals damage. When a certain number of units die this way, they summon a large meteorite that deals damage on impact and turns into a golem.

Axe Throwers

LegendaryHumanTroopRanged

Axe Throwers are a Legendary Human troop in Kingdom Cash that attack from range. These savage warriors revel in battle and grow stronger as the battle goes on. At level 1, they have 1,800 HP and 430 attack. By level 10 (their maximum), that grows to 46,700 HP and 4,300 attack — 25.9× the HP and 10× the attack. The squad grows from 1 to 3 units as it levels up. Their ability is Beast Rage.

Bone Warrior

CommonUndeadTroopMelee

Bone Warrior is a Common Undead troop in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. A soulless skeleton in armor that blindly obeys its Master. What could be better? At level 1, it has 900 HP and 100 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 16,540 HP and 840 attack — 18.4× the HP and 8.4× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up.

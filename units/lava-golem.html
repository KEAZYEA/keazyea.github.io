Lava Golem

EpicMageTroopMelee

Lava Golem is an Epic Mage troop in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. A clump of magma. If disturbed, can easily turn into a furious flaming creature that incinerates everything it touches. At level 1, it has 3,300 HP and 270 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 64,345 HP and 2,340 attack — 19.5× the HP and 8.7× the attack. The squad grows from 1 to 3 units as it levels up. Its ability is Fire Form.

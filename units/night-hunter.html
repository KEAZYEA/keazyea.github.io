Night Hunter

LegendaryUndeadTroopMelee

Night Hunter is a Legendary Undead troop in Kingdom Cash that fights in melee. The true embodiment of evil and a merciless creature that can't be stopped from achieving its only goal: to satisfy its constant hunger. At level 1, it has 4,000 HP and 270 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 91,500 HP and 1,630 attack — 22.9× the HP and 6× the attack. A squad has 1 unit. Its ability is Nightmare.

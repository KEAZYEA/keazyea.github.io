Ashira

BossSpider QueenLevels 1–300

Ashira, the Spider Queen, is a Glorious Hunt boss in Kingdom Clash, from level 1 to 300. It attacks with Basic attack, Explosive Spiders Summon, Battle Spiders Summon, Acid Barrage. Its passive abilities are Thick Shell, Speed and Agility, Chitin Carapace. Pushing it all the way to level 300 takes 37,556,733 damage in total.

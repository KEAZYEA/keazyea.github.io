Sorcerer's Apprentice

CommonMageTroopRanged

Sorcerer's Apprentice is a Common Mage troop in Kingdom Cash that attacks from range. Young magicians still in training by more experienced mentors, but already capable of creating dangerous magic arrows and using them in battle. At level 1, it has 500 HP and 215 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 13,280 HP and 1,650 attack — 26.6× the HP and 7.7× the attack. The squad grows from 3 to 9 units as it levels up.

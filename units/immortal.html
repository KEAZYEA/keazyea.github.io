Immortal

EpicUndeadTroopMeleeArea damage

Immortal is an Epic Undead troop in Kingdom Clash that fights in melee, dealing area damage. One swing of his sword sows death and panic in the enemy's army. At level 1, it has 2,600 HP and 200 attack. By level 10 (its maximum), that grows to 52,440 HP and 1,360 attack — 20.2× the HP and 6.8× the attack. The squad grows from 1 to 3 units as it levels up.

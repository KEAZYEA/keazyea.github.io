About Keazyea's Intelligence Hub

Keazyea's Intelligence Hub is a free companion site for players of Kingdom Cash. It's made and run by Keazyea, and puts unit stats, promo codes, tips and community features in one place.

What you'll find here

  • Troops & heroes guide: every unit's HP, attack, defence and power at each level, ability numbers and hero talents, plus a tool in the app to compare any two units side by side.
  • Promo codes: the latest working codes, and giveaways with real prizes.
  • Tips: team compositions, talents and event strategy.
  • Games: a daily puzzle and quick quiz games about the game's units, with a weekly leaderboard.
  • Community: clan recruitment, friends, tier lists and formations.

Who's behind it

Hello, I'm Keazyea, the creator of this site. The stats on the unit pages come from the game's own data files. You can also find me on YouTube and on Discord, and you're welcome to join my clan in Kingdom Cash (Android).
